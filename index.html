ABOUT THE PROGRAM

What is READ-UP?

READ-UP is a school-based reading support platform designed to provide structured reading practice. It combines reading passages, vocabulary work, comprehension questions, and assessments in one accessible place.

📚

Accessible Materials

Reading texts and activities organized by level.

🧠

Comprehension Skills

Progress Monitoring
